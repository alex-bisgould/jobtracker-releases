Job Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 August 2026 · Last updated 30 August 2026

Job Tracker is a macOS app that runs on your own computer. Your job search — the roles you're tracking, your résumés, your contacts, and the emails you import — is stored on your Mac and is not uploaded to us.

The short version. There is no analytics, no telemetry, and no crash reporting in Job Tracker. No third-party tracking SDK is bundled in the app. We do not sell, rent, or share your data. If you never create an online account, the app sends nothing about you anywhere except to the services you explicitly connect.

1. Who we are

Job Tracker is developed and operated by Alex Bisgould, an independent developer. For anything in this policy, contact abisgould@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your Mac

By default, all of your job-search content is written to a folder on your own computer, normally ~/Library/Application Support/JobTracker. This includes:

  • Jobs, leads, companies, and contacts
  • Résumés, cover letters, offer letters, and any other attachments you add
  • Email and message content you import, including subjects and message bodies
  • Interview notes and transcripts
  • Logs of AI requests you make, including the prompt sent and the response received
  • Your app settings and profiles

None of this is copied to our servers. Deleting the app's data folder deletes it.

We think you should know this precisely rather than in the abstract:

We recommend keeping FileVault enabled. If this matters to you, you can use Job Tracker without connecting a mail account at all.

3. What leaves your Mac, and when

Nothing in this section happens unless you turn on the feature it belongs to.

Online account (optional)

If you create an account or sign in with Google or Apple, we store account and billing metadata in a Supabase database hosted in Canada (ca-central-1). That record can contain: your email address, display name and username, account status, plan or entitlement and its dates, household membership and invites, and counters of AI usage (day, feature, model, token counts, and estimated cost).

It does not contain your jobs, leads, contacts, résumés, attachments, or messages. There is no table in our database for that content, and no feature that uploads it. Cross-machine sync of your job data does not exist in the app.

Google API disclosure

Job Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Specifically: Gmail data is used only to show you your own correspondence about jobs you are tracking; it is not transferred to anyone else, is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train any AI model. It is not read by humans.

AI features

Job Tracker's AI features work on content you choose — a job description you paste, a résumé you want tailored, an email thread you want summarized. Sending that content to an AI model necessarily transmits it.

  • Your own API key. If you supply an Anthropic or OpenAI key, requests go directly from your Mac to that provider. We are not in the path and never see the content. The provider's own privacy terms govern what they do with it.
  • Built-in AI. If a built-in AI plan is offered and you use it, requests pass through a server we operate in order to meter usage against your plan. That server forwards the request to the model provider and streams the answer back. It records only counters — date, feature, model, token counts, and cost. It does not store your prompt or the response.
  • Copy-prompt mode. If no key and no plan is available, the app renders the prompt as text for you to paste elsewhere yourself. Nothing is transmitted.

Other outbound requests

  • Company logos. To display an employer's logo, the app requests an image from Clearbit using that company's domain name. This tells Clearbit which company domains you have looked up, though not who you are.
  • Job posting lookups. If you paste a Greenhouse or Lever job link, the app fetches that public posting from their API.
  • Updates. The app checks GitHub for new releases and downloads them from there.

Feedback

If you rate an AI result, the app may record your rating together with the feature, model, and plan involved. The prompt, the model's response, the text you kept, and any edits you made never leave your device. This sync is currently disabled in the shipping app.

4. Households and shared accounts

A household shares billing and an aggregate usage total — nothing else. A household administrator can see how much AI usage and cost the household has run up. They cannot open your profiles, read your jobs, or see your messages, because that data does not exist on our servers for anyone to read.

5. Children

Job Tracker is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. How long we keep things

Local data stays on your Mac until you delete it. Account records are kept while your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete the account record and its associated rows; usage counters may be retained in aggregate for billing reconciliation.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Use the app entirely offline — an account is optional
  • Disconnect any mail or contacts integration at any time
  • Delete your local data by deleting the app's data folder
  • Revoke Job Tracker's access from your Google account permissions or Apple ID settings at any time
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account record by emailing abisgould@gmail.com

Depending on where you live, you may have additional statutory rights over your personal data. We honour those requests regardless of where you live.

8. Security

Account data is protected by row-level security policies so that one account cannot read another's. Session tokens and AI keys are encrypted on your device. As noted in section 2, mail and iCloud credentials are not. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects how your data is handled, note it in the app's release notes.
